# Code of conduct

We want this project to be welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks or discriminatory language.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Contact the maintainer, [@kashmithnisakya](https://github.com/kashmithnisakya), or use GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) tools. Maintainers may remove content or block people who break these rules.
